Questions

Field work management, answered.

Can a field user use Backline on a phone?

Yes. Backline is responsive for phone, tablet, and desktop use, and can be installed as a web app on supported devices.

What happens when the team loses connection?

On a trusted device where offline access has been enabled, Backline can keep saved workspace data available and sync locally saved changes after a connection returns.

Can the office see changes made in the field?

Yes. Field updates such as job progress, tasks, notes, and permitted files remain part of the shared workspace record.
