Yes. Backline can be configured for service, repair, professional, and other customer-based businesses that need to schedule and track work.

What does the assigned team member see?

Assigned users see the customer, scheduled work, relevant notes, files, and permitted actions based on their role.

Yes. Backline includes a customer portal for shared updates, files, and replies that remain connected to the customer work record.
