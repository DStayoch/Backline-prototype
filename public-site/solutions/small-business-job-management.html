Questions

Small business job management software, answered.

What is job management software for a small business?

It is a shared system for tracking customer work from the first request through scheduling, completion, payment, and follow-up. Backline keeps those steps and their supporting details together.

Can Backline work for a business that is not a trade company?

Yes. Backline supports several business types and uses adaptable workflow language so customer-based small businesses can organize work in a way that fits their day.

Can a small team use Backline from a phone?

Yes. Backline is designed for phone, tablet, and desktop use, and can be installed as a web app. Offline access can also be enabled on a trusted device.
