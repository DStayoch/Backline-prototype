Quick tour

Run your shop in Backline

Use these four steps whenever you need a simple refresher. Nothing here changes your data.

  1. 1
    Start with Home

    See what needs attention, today's work, money waiting, and the next setup task.

  2. 2
    Add the customer and job

    Enter the caller's details once. Backline keeps the customer, job, messages, and history connected.

  3. 3
    Follow the next step

    Open a job and use its suggested next step to book it, start field work, send an estimate, or record payment.

  4. 4
    Close the loop with the customer

    Send estimates, share the customer portal, invoice the work, and record the payment in one place.
