# Validation Plan

## Goal

Validate that small HVAC/plumbing shops will pay for missed-call recovery before building a full field-service platform.

## Target

Interview 20 owners or office admins.

Prioritize:

- 1-10 tech residential HVAC/plumbing shops
- Shops with no dedicated full-time dispatcher
- Shops using Google Calendar, QuickBooks, paper, Jobber, Housecall Pro, or spreadsheets

## Interview Questions

1. How many inbound calls do you get in a normal week?
2. How many do you miss?
3. What happens when a call is missed?
4. Who follows up with customers?
5. How quickly do you usually respond?
6. What is an average booked job worth?
7. What tools do you use today?
8. What do you dislike about those tools?
9. Would you let software text back a missed caller automatically?
10. What would make that feel risky?
11. If this booked 2-3 extra jobs per month, what would it be worth?
12. Would you try it if setup took under 15 minutes?

## Strong Validation Signals

- Owner knows missed calls are costing revenue
- Owner can estimate value per booked job
- Owner currently follows up manually or inconsistently
- Owner is willing to connect phone/SMS or forward missed calls
- Owner will pay at least $79/month for a working version
- Owner asks when they can try it

## Weak Validation Signals

- They rarely miss calls
- They already have a staffed office
- They only care about full FSM replacement
- They distrust automated texting
- They need deep integrations before testing

## Concierge MVP

Before building the full system:

- Set up a dedicated phone number
- Forward missed calls to the service
- Use human-assisted SMS responses
- Log jobs in a simple dashboard or spreadsheet
- Manually notify owner
- Track booked jobs and revenue

This tests demand without overbuilding.

## Landing Page Copy

Headline:

Never lose a job because you missed the phone.

Subhead:

An AI back office for small HVAC and plumbing shops that texts missed callers, captures job details, books appointments, and follows up until the work is won.

Primary CTA:

Recover my missed calls

Secondary CTA:

See how it works

Proof Points:

- Texts missed callers in under 30 seconds
- Turns calls into clean job cards
- Follows up on estimates and invoices
- Built for owners still working in the field

## 30-Day Plan

### Week 1

- Interview 10 owners
- Build landing page
- Create clickable dashboard prototype
- Define SMS scripts

### Week 2

- Interview 10 more owners
- Run concierge test with 2-3 shops
- Manually handle missed-call text-back
- Track recovered jobs

### Week 3

- Build real inbox, customer, and job workflow
- Add SMS provider integration
- Add owner notifications

### Week 4

- Add booking, follow-up automations, and payment-link tracking
- Convert concierge users into paid pilots
- Decide whether to deepen HVAC/plumbing workflows or stay horizontal across trades
