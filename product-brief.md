# Field-Service Back Office MVP

## Working Positioning

The simplest AI-powered back office for 1-10 person HVAC and plumbing shops.

The first wedge is missed-call recovery: when an owner or dispatcher misses a call, the product texts the customer back, captures the job details, books the appointment, and keeps following up until the job is scheduled, paid, or closed.

## Ideal Customer Profile

- U.S. residential HVAC or plumbing contractor
- 1-10 technicians
- Owner is still involved in field work
- Uses phone, SMS, Google Calendar, QuickBooks, and Stripe/Square or manual card payments
- Misses calls during busy hours, after hours, or while on jobs
- Does not want a heavy ServiceTitan-style implementation

## Core Pain

Small trade shops lose revenue because the person responsible for answering calls is often also dispatching, quoting, doing jobs, or managing crews. Missed calls become missed jobs, estimates go stale, invoices sit unpaid, and customer follow-up depends on memory.

## Product Promise

Never lose a job because nobody answered the phone.

## MVP Scope

### Must Have

- Missed-call text-back
- Customer intake by SMS
- Job request record
- Simple scheduling link or dispatcher calendar
- Owner/admin dashboard
- Technician job detail view
- Estimate and invoice links
- Payment status tracking
- Automated reminders and follow-up

### Should Have

- Job categories for HVAC and plumbing
- Customer history
- Call/source tracking
- Review request after completion
- Basic revenue and conversion dashboard

### Later

- QuickBooks Online sync
- Equipment history
- Maintenance plans
- Financing
- Inventory and truck stock
- Supplier integrations
- Route optimization
- Full mobile technician app

## Initial Workflow

1. Customer calls.
2. If the call is missed, the app sends an SMS within 30 seconds.
3. AI asks for name, address, issue, urgency, and preferred time.
4. Customer picks a slot or requests callback.
5. Owner sees a clean job card with customer details and suggested priority.
6. Owner confirms, assigns, or reschedules.
7. After the visit, the app sends estimate, invoice, payment link, and review request.
8. If the customer does not respond, the app follows up automatically.

## Differentiation

- Built for the owner who is still in the truck
- Starts with revenue recovery, not generic admin software
- Setup in minutes, not weeks
- Phone/SMS first
- Transparent pricing
- Expands into job management only after earning daily use

## Pricing Hypothesis

- Solo: $79/month
- Crew: $199/month
- Growth: $399/month
- AI receptionist add-on or included usage tier
- Payment processing margin later

## Success Metrics

- Missed calls recovered
- Jobs booked from missed calls
- Estimate follow-up conversion
- Time from call to booked appointment
- Revenue attributed to recovered jobs
- Owner weekly active usage

## First 10 Customer Test

Find 10 HVAC/plumbing owners with 1-10 techs. Ask for:

- Number of missed calls per week
- Close rate on answered calls
- Current follow-up process
- Tools used today
- Willingness to forward missed calls or connect phone/SMS
- Whether recovered revenue would justify $79-$199/month

The product should not be expanded until at least 3-5 test users confirm that missed-call recovery creates obvious value.
